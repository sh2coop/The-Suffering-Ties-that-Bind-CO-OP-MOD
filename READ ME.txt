THE SUFFERING: TIES THAT BIND on PC, with co-op (test build)

HOW TO START

  1. Put your Xbox disc image of the game (the .iso file) into the folder "drop xbox iso here".
  2. Double-click "Build Recomp".
  3. It asks whether to make HD textures too (see below). Then it builds the game (a few minutes,
     only this once) and the game starts.

That is all. From then on, double-click "The Suffering 2" to play.

GOOD TO KNOW

  - Your image is a .7z, .zip or .rar? Unpack it first, the .iso is inside.
  - Windows asks once if the game may use the network. Say Allow if you want to play online.
  - No game data comes with this folder. You need your own copy of the game.

CO-OP

  In the game: Options, then "Display and Co-op".
  - Split screen: two controllers on one PC. Controller 1 is Player 1.
  - Online, no port forwarding needed:
      1. The host picks "Host online". His screen shows "Your code". He sends it to his friend.
      2. The friend picks "Join online", presses A on "Host's code" and enters it (Ctrl+V pastes).
         His screen then shows his own code. He sends that back.
      3. The host presses A on "Your code" and enters the friend's code. The friend comes in.
    If a screen says the router gives every connection a new port, forward UDP port 27025 on
    the host's router instead and enter the plain address.

  - Player 2 stuck or left behind? Hold A+B+X+Y for five seconds (keyboard: T) and he is beside Player 1.
  - Either player can pause. Leaving an online game: hold BACK (keyboard: Backspace) for two seconds.

KEYBOARD

  Arrows = d-pad, Enter = START, Backspace = BACK, Space or J = A, K = B, U = X, I = Y,
  O = white, P = black, W A S D = move, Q and E = triggers, numpad 8 2 4 6 = look.

HD TEXTURES

  Build Recomp can upscale every texture of your own game image 4x with an AI upscaler (Real-ESRGAN,
  fetched once from its GitHub page). It needs about 3 GB free and runs on the graphics card:
  about 15 to 30 minutes on a fast one, longer on an older one. Close its window to stop; the next
  Build Recomp continues where it stopped. Already built the game? Double-click Build Recomp again
  and say Yes to the HD textures: the game itself is not built again.
  In the game: Options, Display and Co-op, HD textures On or Off.

UPDATES

  Double-click "Update". It fetches the newest version when there is one.
  The version you have is in VERSION.txt.

SOMETHING WENT WRONG?

  Zip the "Logs" folder (it is right here, beside "The Suffering 2") and send it.
  It has one file for every time the game was started, and the crash reports.
